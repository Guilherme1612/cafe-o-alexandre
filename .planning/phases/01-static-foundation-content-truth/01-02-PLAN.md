<objective>
Render the stable semantic Astro shell and warm visual foundation from the shared content object, then prove the generated HTML preserves content truth.

Purpose: VIS-05 and ABOUT-03 need a useful no-JavaScript page that never turns missing facts into fake actions, while UX-03 needs the approved burgundy/charcoal/gold/cream system ready for later sections.
Output: `index.astro`, one global stylesheet, and a static-output assertion covering landmarks, placeholders, palette hooks, and sponsor/invention guardrails.
</objective>

<prohibitions>
- Do not put an address, phone, hours, social handle, offering, map destination, image filename, sponsor name, sponsor logo, or sponsor phone directly in the Astro template or CSS.
- Do not emit an `href`, `tel:`, WhatsApp URL, Maps URL, click handler, fake disabled button, or icon-only action for any unresolved placeholder.
- Do not add contact-action, map, gallery, third-party font, animation, gradient, or hydrated-island behavior in this foundation shell.
</prohibitions>
