# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local visitors checking the café on a phone, usually with an immediate practical goal: recognize the place, call it, or plan a visit.

## Product Purpose

Make Café o Alexandre feel like a real neighborhood café within the first few seconds, then make the next practical action obvious without inventing unverified business facts.

## Brand Personality

Warm, assured, tactile. The tone should feel like a well-kept counter and a handwritten recommendation: welcoming without being cute, elegant without being formal, confident without corporate polish.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS landing page, a crowded restaurant template, a sponsor advertisement, or a centered hero with interchangeable cards. The supplied TORRIÉ image is palette and atmosphere inspiration only; its branding, cup, layout, and phone context are not café content.

## Design Principles

- Put the café and the next action first.
- Let verified information earn visual emphasis; let missing information stay honest and legible.
- Use one memorable composition instead of a pile of generic cards.
- Make touch, contrast, keyboard focus, and reduced motion part of the brand experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: keyboard-visible focus, strong contrast, descriptive labels, 44px+ touch targets, readable type, responsive wrapping at narrow widths and 200% zoom, and reduced-motion support.
